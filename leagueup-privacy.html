League Up — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 May 2026

League Up ("the app") is published by George Gordon. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and what your rights are.

What we collect

  • Account info. When you sign in with Apple or email, we receive your Apple ID identifier or email and a display name. We never see your Apple ID password.
  • Activity you create in the app. Leagues you create or join, check-ins you log (numeric values, photos if a league requires photo proof), streaks, scores, and feed events.
  • Push notification tokens. If you grant notification permission, your device's push token is stored so we can send streak reminders and league activity alerts.
  • Health data (iOS only, optional). If you create a steps / workout / sleep league and grant Apple Health permission, we read those metrics so we can auto-log your check-in. This data is read on your device and written to your league entries — it is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Purchase status. If you subscribe to League Up Pro, we receive an entitlement flag from RevenueCat indicating active membership. We do not see your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple.
  • Crash and error reports. If the app crashes, an anonymised stack trace is sent to Sentry so we can fix it. No message contents or personal data are included.

What we do not collect

  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, microphone, location, or any other system data not listed above.
  • We do not run any third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

Where your data lives

League Up uses Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) hosted in europe-west2 (London). Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. RevenueCat handles subscription state via Apple's StoreKit. Push notifications are routed through the Expo Push service to Apple's APNs.

How long we keep it

Account data persists until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your user record, every league membership, every check-in, and every feed event you authored are removed within 30 days. Aggregate league data (e.g. a league you created that other people joined) is retained as part of those leagues' history.

Your rights

  • Access. Request a copy of your data by emailing george.gordon007@gmail.com.
  • Deletion. Delete your account from Profile → Delete account in the app. This is irreversible and permanently removes your data.
  • Correction. Update your display name and other profile data anytime in Profile.
  • Object to processing. Stop using the app and your data will only be retained for the deletion window above.

Sharing with third parties

We share data only with the infrastructure providers necessary to run the app:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, storage
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, push notifications, in-app purchases, HealthKit
  • RevenueCat — subscription state
  • Expo — push token routing
  • Sentry — anonymised crash reports

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any other parties.

Children

League Up is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete their account.

Changes

If this policy materially changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify active users in-app.
